Part 1

Executive Strategy & Goals

Mariah Travel is a purpose-driven company specializing in small-group educational adventures. The objective of this redesign is to transition the digital presence from a standard transactional booking site to an expert-led authority platform that prioritizes relationships, conservation, and meaningful connections.

Core Business Goals

  • ✓
    Meaningful Travel Immerse travelers (35-70+) in authentic culture, nature, wildlife, and local communities.
  • ✓
    Lasting Relationships Build a loyal community of travelers, partners, and specialty interest groups over one-time bookings.
  • ✓
    Conservation Support Design experiences that directly benefit local people and sustainable tourism efforts.
  • ✓
    Trusted Expert Positioning Leverage decades of field experience, ecological expertise, and destination knowledge.

Strategic Shift Focus

Transitioning focus from generic tourism to authority-led experiences.

Part 3

User Journey Flows

People rarely browse in only one way. We must support multiple entry points. Our goal is to move visitors from curiosity to a conversation, bypassing the "Expedia-like" impulse buy.

🔍 Flow A: Destination-Driven (The Reassurance Seeker)

Target: Mature Explorer (Age 50-70+). Highly motivated but needs safety and trust established first.

🏠 1. Home / Hero "Small Group Culture & Nature Journeys Around the World"
→
↓
📍 2. "Discover Cuba" Reviews "Is it legal? Is it safe?" FAQs & Testimonials.
→
↓
👥 3. The Mariah Difference Sees NWF Award, Decades of Experience. Trust established.
→
↓
💬 4. Talk With Us Submits detailed inquiry form for a conversation.

🌱 Flow B: Theme-Driven (The Niche Enthusiast)

Target: Specialty interests (Birding, Photography, Art/Music). Focuses on the activity over the location.

🔎 1. Organic Search Googles "Birding Cuba in Spring" (Blog/Resource hit).
→
↓
🦉 2. Birding Experience Page Sees itineraries tailored to wildlife and photography.
→
↓
📥 3. Lead Capture Downloads "5 Birds You Can Only See in Cuba" PDF.
→
↓
✉ 4. Email Nurture Receives relationship-building emails leading to booking.

Part 5

Trust & Conversion Engine

To move away from transactional OTA (Online Travel Agency) templates, Mariah Travel relies on "Truth Signals". People buy people, and they need deep reassurance for educational, high-ticket adventures.

The Voice of the Traveler

Specific testimonials integrated contextually (e.g., Birding quotes on the Birding page, not hidden on a generic 'Reviews' page).
