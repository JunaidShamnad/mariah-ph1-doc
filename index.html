This is the core deliverable of Week 1. To ensure scalability, we are shifting the nomenclature from restrictive terms ("Cuba Tours") to expandable frameworks ("Destinations", "Experiences"). Interact with the sitemap below to explore the proposed hierarchy.

  • 🏆 The Mariah Difference +

    • • Testimonials
  • 📧 Connect / Contact Us
💡

Strategic Rationale

  • ✓
    Scalability: By using "Destinations" instead of hardcoding "Cuba Tours", the UI framework supports adding Thailand or Colombia seamlessly in the future.
  • ✓
    Multiple Entry Points: Users don't browse just one way. The architecture allows them to find trips by Geography (Peru), by Passion (Birding), or by Style (Small Group).
  • ✓
    Authority Building: "The Mariah Difference" dedicates top-level space to trust signals, prominently featuring the National Wildlife Federation recognition and safety data.
